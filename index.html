📖 Personal Accounting: The Cashbook Principle

A single-entry cashbook records immediate chronological cash inflows and outflows to maintain liquidity awareness and prevent deficit spending:

  • The Power of Manual Tracking: Unlike automated bank-sync tools where expenses pass unnoticed, manual entry creates behavioral mindfulness, directly curbing impulse consumption by up to 25%.
  • The 50/30/20 Cashflow Rule: Allocate 50% of net income to Essential Needs (rent, utilities, groceries), 30% to Discretionary Lifestyle wants (dining, recreation), and a minimum of 20% to Wealth Building & Savings.
  • Net Cashflow vs Net Worth: Wealth begins with consistent monthly cashflow surpluses. Protecting a positive spread between monthly inflows and outflows is the primary driver of financial security.

Accounting references: Warren E. & Tyagi A. (2005) All Your Worth: The Ultimate Lifetime Money Plan; Weygandt J. (2018) Accounting Principles.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does this cashbook protect my financial privacy?

The application runs 100% client-side without bank logins or remote database syncing. All transactions, incomes, and balances reside exclusively inside your device's browser memory.

How is the net savings rate computed?

The savings rate measures remaining net cashflow as a percentage of total income: (Total Income - Total Expenses) ÷ Total Income × 100.

Can I export my financial ledger to CSV or JSON?

Yes. You can download your full ledger as a spreadsheet-compatible CSV file or a JSON backup file to archive or analyze in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

Does this tool require bank credentials or account linking?

No. This tool operates as an intentional manual cashbook. No banking passwords, account numbers, or personal identifying credentials are ever requested.
